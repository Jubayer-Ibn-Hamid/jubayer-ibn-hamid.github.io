I work in artificial intelligence and reinforcement learning.

I am currently a PhD student at Stanford University and a Student Researcher at Google. I am affiliated with Stanford Artificial Intelligence Laboratory (SAIL), where my research is advised by Dorsa Sadigh and Chelsea Finn.

Previously, I studied mathematical physics as an undergraduate at Stanford University. Outside of AI, I am interested in pure mathematics, especially abstract algebra and neighbouring fields.
