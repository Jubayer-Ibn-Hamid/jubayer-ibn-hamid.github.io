# Fonts for Science and AI

The article hosts its fonts locally; no external font service is required.

- `libertinus/`: unmodified WOFF2 files from [Libertinus 7.051](https://github.com/alerque/libertinus/releases/tag/v7.051). Serif regular and italic match the document's `libertinus` text family; Math and Mono cover equations and the email address.
- `computer-modern/`: CMU Serif regular from [Computer Modern Web Fonts](https://github.com/dreampulse/computer-modern-web-font/tree/master/font/Serif). Used for the document's explicit Computer Modern section heading override, with CSS small caps.

Both font families are distributed under the SIL Open Font License. Their licenses are included in their respective directories.
